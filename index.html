Built with compassion, not shame.

Daily Reset was created by a clinical mental health counselor who wanted practical mental health skills to be easier to access outside the therapy room.

The app is grounded in a simple belief: people do not have to earn their worth before they begin growing. You can start from where you are, practice what helps, and keep building a life that feels more aligned with who you want to become.

Daily Reset provides educational and supportive mental health tools. It is not a replacement for therapy, diagnosis, crisis services, or medical treatment.

Frequently asked questions

A few things to know before getting started with Daily Reset.

Is Daily Reset therapy?

No. Daily Reset provides educational tools and guided mental health skills. It does not replace therapy, diagnosis, crisis care, or treatment from a qualified professional.

Who is Daily Reset designed for?

Daily Reset is designed for adults who want practical support with emotional awareness, anxiety, overwhelm, self-doubt, relationships, burnout, and everyday stress.

How long do the exercises take?

Many tools can be used in only a few minutes. Other skills can be revisited over time as you become more comfortable using them.

Can I use Daily Reset during a crisis?

Daily Reset is not a crisis service. If you may be in immediate danger or are thinking about harming yourself or someone else, contact emergency services or a crisis service in your area.

Where can I access Daily Reset?

Daily Reset is available through the web app, with mobile app download links being added as public releases become available.
