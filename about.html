About Daily Reset & Next Mountain Ventures LLC

Daily Reset is a digital wellness application developed and operated by Next Mountain Ventures LLC, a Utah limited liability company.

Next Mountain Ventures LLC creates digital tools, educational content, and media designed to support mental wellness, personal growth, and practical everyday skills.

Company Overview

Next Mountain Ventures LLC is a Utah domestic limited liability company. The company develops and publishes digital wellness products, educational content, mobile applications, publishing, and media focused on mental wellness, personal development, and self-improvement.

Daily Reset is the company's digital wellness product and brand. Next Mountain Ventures LLC owns and operates the Daily Reset app and this website at https://dailyreset-app.com.

About Daily Reset

Daily Reset helps people slow down, understand what they are experiencing, and choose one practical step that supports the person they want to become. It offers daily mood check-ins, practical mental health skills, short guided audio, and interactive practice.

Daily Reset provides educational and supportive wellness tools. It is not therapy, counseling, medical care, a crisis service, or a substitute for treatment from a qualified professional.
